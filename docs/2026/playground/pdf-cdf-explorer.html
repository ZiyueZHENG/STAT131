PDF and CDF Explorer

Type a simple nonnegative function, choose an interval, and click Draw PDF and CDF.

The function will be numerically normalized so that the total area under the PDF is 1.

Examples you can try:

  • 1
  • x^2
  • exp(-x)
  • exp(-x^2 / 2)
  • x >= 0 and x <= 1 ? 1 : 0
